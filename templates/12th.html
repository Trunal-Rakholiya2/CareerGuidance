🎨 Design & Creativity (After 12th PCM)

Design & Creativity is a career stream for students who love art, visuals, innovation, and original thinking. PCM students fit well because modern design combines logic, creativity, and technology.

👉 Perfect for students who prefer skills and creativity over memorization.

Who Should Choose This Stream?
  • Love drawing, sketching, or designing
  • Enjoy art, digital creativity, or visual thinking
  • Want a career based on skill + portfolio
  • PCM students interested in creative technology
  • Prefer practical work over theory
Main Design Degrees After 12th PCM
Bachelor of Design (B.Des)

Core professional design degree

B.Tech / B.Des in Design Engineering

Design + engineering concepts

Interaction Design (UI/UX)

App & website experience design

Industrial / Product Design

Designing real-world physical products

🔬 Science & Research (After 12th PCM)

Science & Research is a core academic career path for PCM students who enjoy concepts, logic, experiments, and deep subject understanding. This branch focuses on discovering, analyzing, and explaining how the physical world works.

👉 Best for students who prefer knowledge, theory, and long-term academic growth.

Who Should Choose This Branch?
  • Students who enjoy Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics
  • Those who like formulas, derivations, and concepts
  • Interested in research, teaching, or scientific roles
  • Comfortable with higher studies (M.Sc / PhD)
  • Prefer depth of knowledge over quick placements
Main Branches in Science & Research (PCM)
Physics

Energy, motion, electronics, space & research

Chemistry

Chemicals, reactions, materials & lab work

Mathematics

Logic, numbers, proofs & abstract thinking

Statistics

Data, probability & analytical modeling

Data Science (Science Route)

Maths + statistics + research computing

💻 Technology & Engineering (After 12th PCM)

Technology & Engineering is the most popular career path for PCM students. It focuses on applying scientific principles to build machines, software, structures, and systems.

👉 Best for students who love problem-solving, innovation, and practical application.

Who Should Choose This Stream?
  • Love technology, gadgets, or machines
  • Enjoy coding, building, or fixing things
  • Want a career with high industry demand
  • Good at logical reasoning and mathematics
  • Interested in how things work (software or hardware)
Main Engineering Branches
Computer Science (CSE)

Software, coding, AI & web development

Mechanical Engineering

Machines, automobiles & robotics

Civil Engineering

Construction, infrastructure & design

Electrical Engineering

Power systems, circuits & electronics

Electronics (ECE)

Communication systems & microchips

Chemical Engineering

Chemical processes & manufacturing

Aerospace Engineering

Aircraft, spacecraft & space technology

Automobile Engineering

Vehicle design, engines & manufacturing

“PCMB is for students who want options, not limitations.”

What is PCMB?

PCMB is a science stream that includes Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology. It is considered the most flexible science combination because it allows students to choose careers in medical, engineering, research, and interdisciplinary fields.
