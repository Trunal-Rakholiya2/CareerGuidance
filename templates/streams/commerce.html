Core Commerce Degrees
What are Core Commerce Degrees?

Core Commerce degrees are the foundation courses of the commerce stream. They focus on business, money flow, accounting systems, finance, taxation, economics, and trade.

These degrees help students understand how companies earn money, manage expenses, pay taxes, take financial decisions, and operate in markets. Almost every business and organization depends on commerce professionals.

Main Core Commerce Degrees
  • Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) – General commerce degree covering accounting, economics, business law, taxation, management, and finance.
  • B.Com (Honours) – In-depth and advanced version of B.Com, suitable for students interested in higher studies and research.
  • B.Com Accounting & Finance – Strong focus on accounting, auditing, financial statements, and corporate finance.
  • B.Com Banking & Insurance – Banking operations, insurance systems, financial services, and risk concepts.
  • B.Com Taxation – Income tax, GST, tax laws, compliance, and tax planning.
  • B.Com Financial Markets – Stock market, mutual funds, securities, and investment basics.
  • B.Com International Business – Export-import procedures, foreign trade, international finance, and global markets.
  • B.A / B.Sc Economics – Study of money, demand & supply, economic growth, inflation, and government policies.
  • B.Sc Finance – Corporate finance, investment analysis, financial planning, and risk management.
Eligibility & Course Duration
  • Passed 12th Commerce from a recognized board
  • Mathematics is preferred for Economics & Finance courses
  • Course Duration: 3 Years
Subjects You Will Commonly Study
  • Financial Accounting
  • Business Economics
  • Cost & Management Accounting
  • Business Law & Corporate Law
  • Income Tax & GST
  • Banking & Financial Systems
  • Business Statistics & Mathematics
Who Should Choose Core Commerce?
  • Students who like numbers, calculations, and analysis
  • Those interested in finance, banking, taxation, or business
  • Students planning professional courses like CA, CS, CMA
  • Those aiming for government or corporate jobs
  • Students who want multiple career options after graduation
Career Options After Core Commerce
  • Accountant
  • Auditor
  • Banking Executive / Officer
  • Tax Consultant
  • Finance Executive
  • Business Analyst
  • Accounts Manager (with experience)
Private Sector Scope

Core commerce graduates are required in almost every private organization, including companies, banks, startups, audit firms, finance companies, and multinational corporations.

Government Job Scope
  • Banking Exams (IBPS, SBI, RBI – after graduation)
  • SSC Exams (CGL, CHSL)
  • UPSC & State PSC
  • Income Tax & Audit Departments
Average Salary (India)
  • Freshers: ₹2.5 – 4 LPA
  • After experience / certifications: ₹6 – 12+ LPA
  • With CA / MBA / CMA: Salary increases significantly
Higher Study & Certification Options
  • M.Com (Master of Commerce)
  • MBA (Finance, Marketing, HR, Business Analytics)
  • CA / CS / CMA
  • Finance & Investment Certifications
Skills You Should Develop
  • Basic accounting & financial knowledge
  • Numerical and analytical skills
  • Communication & business understanding
  • Excel, accounting software, and basic data skills

“Core Commerce builds the financial backbone of every business and economy.”

Management Degrees (Commerce)
What are Management Degrees?

Management degrees focus on planning, organizing, leading, and managing people, business operations, and companies. These courses are designed to develop leadership, decision-making, and business strategy skills.

Unlike Core Commerce (which focuses on accounts and finance), Management degrees focus more on how businesses run, grow, and compete in real life.

Main Management Degrees in Commerce
  • Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) – The most popular management degree covering marketing, finance, HR, operations, and strategy.
  • Bachelor of Management Studies (BMS) – More analytical and theory-oriented management degree, preferred by top universities.
  • BBA Marketing – Focus on advertising, branding, sales, and consumer behavior.
  • BBA Finance – Corporate finance, budgeting, investments, and financial decision-making.
  • BBA Human Resource (HR) – Hiring, training, employee relations, and organizational behavior.
  • BBA International Business – Global trade, international markets, and multinational business management.
  • BBA Entrepreneurship – Starting and managing your own business, startups, and innovation.
  • BBA Digital Marketing – Online marketing, social media, SEO, and digital branding.
  • BBA Business Analytics – Data-based decision making, analytics, and business intelligence.
  • BBA FinTech – Finance + technology, digital payments, and financial innovations.
Eligibility & Course Duration
  • Passed 12th Commerce (students from any stream may apply in some colleges)
  • Mathematics preferred for Analytics & Finance specializations
  • Course Duration: 3 Years
Subjects You Will Commonly Study
  • Principles of Management
  • Marketing Management
  • Financial Management
  • Human Resource Management
  • Operations & Supply Chain Management
  • Business Communication
  • Business Analytics & Data Basics
Who Should Choose Management Degrees?
  • Students interested in leadership and decision-making
  • Those who prefer teamwork, presentations, and practical work
  • Students planning MBA in the future
  • Those interested in corporate roles and business growth
  • Students with good communication and interpersonal skills
Career Options After Management Degrees
  • Management Trainee
  • Marketing Executive
  • HR Executive
  • Business Analyst
  • Operations Executive
  • Sales & Growth Manager
Private Sector Scope

Management graduates are highly demanded in private companies, startups, multinational corporations, consulting firms, banks, and e-commerce companies.

Government Job Scope
  • Management roles in PSUs (after MBA)
  • Banking & SSC exams
  • Administrative roles through UPSC / State PSC
Average Salary (India)
  • Freshers: ₹3 – 6 LPA
  • With experience / MBA: ₹8 – 20+ LPA
Higher Study & Career Growth
  • MBA (Marketing, Finance, HR, Analytics, Strategy)
  • PGDM & Management Certifications
  • International Business & Leadership Programs
Skills You Should Develop
  • Communication & presentation skills
  • Leadership and teamwork
  • Problem-solving and decision-making
  • Data understanding & business tools

“Managers turn ideas into action and businesses into success.”

Professional Commerce Courses
What are Professional Commerce Courses?

Professional Commerce courses are skill-intensive, certification-based career programs that focus on real-world accounting, finance, taxation, corporate law, and cost management.

These courses are considered high-value and high-respect careers in commerce. They require discipline, consistency, and long-term dedication, but offer strong career growth and salary potential.

Main Professional Commerce Courses
  • Chartered Accountant (CA) – Focuses on accounting, auditing, taxation, financial reporting, and corporate finance.
  • Company Secretary (CS) – Deals with corporate law, company compliance, governance, and legal documentation.
  • Cost & Management Accountant (CMA) – Focuses on cost control, budgeting, performance management, and strategic finance.
Course Structure & Duration
  • Professional courses are divided into multiple levels
  • Average completion time: 3 – 5 years (depends on student progress)
  • Articleship / Practical training is mandatory
Eligibility
  • Passed 12th Commerce (Maths recommended)
  • Can be pursued along with graduation (B.Com / BBA)
  • Strong basics in accounting and business concepts required
Subjects You Will Study
  • Financial Accounting & Reporting
  • Direct Tax & GST
  • Audit & Assurance
  • Corporate & Business Law
  • Cost Accounting & Strategic Management
  • Financial Management
Who Should Choose Professional Courses?
  • Students with strong interest in accounts, finance, and law
  • Those who are disciplined, patient, and self-motivated
  • Students aiming for high-level professional roles
  • Those comfortable with long study duration and exams
Career Options After Completion
  • Chartered Accountant / Cost Accountant / Company Secretary
  • Auditor & Tax Consultant
  • Finance Manager / Compliance Officer
  • Chief Financial Officer (CFO) – with experience
  • Independent Practice / Consultancy
Private Sector Scope

Professional commerce experts are required in audit firms, corporate houses, banks, startups, multinational companies, consulting firms, and financial institutions.

Government Job Scope
  • Income Tax & Audit Departments
  • Public Sector Units (PSUs)
  • Regulatory bodies & government financial offices
Average Salary (India)
  • Freshers: ₹6 – 10 LPA
  • With experience: ₹12 – 25+ LPA
  • Top professionals & consultants earn significantly more
Higher Growth & Opportunities
  • Independent practice & consultancy
  • International certifications & global roles
  • Leadership roles like Finance Head / CFO
Skills You Must Build
  • Strong accounting & financial knowledge
  • Legal understanding & compliance skills
  • Analytical thinking & accuracy
  • Time management & discipline

“Professional commerce courses turn students into industry experts.”

Law Degrees with Commerce
What are Law Degrees with Commerce?

Law degrees with commerce combine business knowledge and legal education. These integrated programs prepare students to handle legal matters related to companies, finance, taxation, and corporate governance.

This path is ideal for students who are interested in law, rules, regulations, contracts, and corporate decision-making, along with strong business understanding.

Main Law Degrees with Commerce
  • B.Com + LLB (5 Years) – Combines commerce subjects with core law subjects such as corporate law, taxation law, and business regulations.
  • BBA + LLB (5 Years) – Focuses on management + law, suitable for corporate legal roles and business advisory positions.
Eligibility & Course Duration
  • Passed 12th Commerce from a recognized board
  • Admission mostly through entrance exams (CLAT / university exams)
  • Course Duration: 5 Years
Subjects You Will Study
  • Business & Corporate Law
  • Contract Law
  • Company Law
  • Taxation Law
  • Constitutional Law
  • Management & Business Studies
  • Legal Research & Drafting
Who Should Choose Law with Commerce?
  • Students interested in law, rules, and legal systems
  • Those who enjoy reading, analysis, and argumentation
  • Students aiming for corporate law and advisory roles
  • Those who want a combination of business + legal careers
Career Options After Law with Commerce
  • Corporate Lawyer
  • Legal Advisor
  • Compliance Officer
  • Company Legal Consultant
  • Tax & Corporate Law Specialist
Private Sector Scope

Graduates are hired by law firms, corporate houses, banks, multinational companies, consulting firms, and legal advisory services.

Government Job Scope
  • Judicial Services (after further exams)
  • Legal Officer in PSUs
  • Government Law Departments
  • Regulatory Authorities
Average Salary (India)
  • Freshers: ₹4 – 8 LPA
  • With experience: ₹10 – 20+ LPA
  • Top corporate lawyers earn significantly more
Higher Study & Career Growth
  • LLM (Master of Laws)
  • Specialization in Corporate, Tax, or International Law
  • Senior legal advisory & partnership roles
Skills You Should Develop
  • Legal research & drafting skills
  • Analytical and logical thinking
  • Strong communication & argument skills
  • Understanding of business laws & compliance

“Law with commerce creates experts who protect and guide businesses.”

Banking, Insurance & Trade
What are Banking, Insurance & Trade Degrees?

Banking, Insurance & Trade degrees focus on financial systems, banking operations, insurance services, trade, and money management. These courses prepare students for roles in banks, financial institutions, insurance companies, and trade organizations.

This career path is suitable for students who prefer stable jobs, structured work, customer interaction, and financial responsibility.

Main Degrees in Banking, Insurance & Trade
  • B.Com Banking – Banking systems, financial institutions, lending, deposits, and customer services.
  • BBA Banking & Insurance – Management + banking and insurance operations.
  • B.Com Insurance – Insurance policies, risk management, underwriting, and claims processing.
  • B.Com Foreign Trade – Export-import procedures, trade documentation, and international business operations.
  • Export–Import Management – Global trade management, logistics, customs, and foreign exchange.
Eligibility & Course Duration
  • Passed 12th Commerce from a recognized board
  • Mathematics preferred for banking and finance-related courses
  • Course Duration: 3 Years
Subjects You Will Commonly Study
  • Banking Laws & Practices
  • Principles of Insurance
  • Financial Institutions & Markets
  • Foreign Trade Procedures
  • Risk Management
  • International Finance
  • Business Communication
Who Should Choose Banking, Insurance & Trade?
  • Students interested in banking and financial services
  • Those who prefer structured and stable career paths
  • Students with good numerical and communication skills
  • Those aiming for bank exams and financial institutions
Career Options After Completion
  • Banking Officer / Clerk
  • Insurance Executive / Officer
  • Trade & Export Executive
  • Relationship Manager
  • Forex & Trade Analyst
Private Sector Scope

Graduates are hired by private banks, insurance companies, NBFCs, export-import firms, logistics companies, and multinational trade organizations.

Government Job Scope
  • Banking Exams (IBPS, SBI, RBI)
  • Insurance Exams (LIC, AAI, UIIC)
  • Trade & Customs Departments
  • Public Sector Banks & Financial Institutions
Average Salary (India)
  • Freshers: ₹3 – 5 LPA
  • With experience: ₹6 – 12+ LPA
  • Government bank jobs offer stable growth & benefits
Higher Study & Growth Options
  • M.Com / MBA (Banking, Finance, International Business)
  • Professional banking & insurance certifications
  • International trade & logistics specialization
Skills You Should Develop
  • Financial & numerical understanding
  • Customer handling & communication skills
  • Knowledge of banking software & processes
  • Basic understanding of trade documentation

“Banking and trade keep the financial system of the nation moving.”

Modern & Specialized Commerce Degrees
What are Modern & Specialized Commerce Degrees?

Modern & Specialized Commerce degrees are designed to match current industry needs, technology-driven business models, and digital economies. These courses combine commerce fundamentals with technology, data, and modern tools.

These programs are ideal for students who want future-ready careers beyond traditional accounting and management roles.

Main Modern & Specialized Commerce Degrees
  • BBA Business Analytics – Data-driven business decision making, analytics tools, and performance analysis.
  • B.Com Data Analytics – Commerce + data interpretation, reporting, and business insights.
  • BBA FinTech – Finance with technology, digital payments, blockchain basics, and financial innovation.
  • B.Com Financial Technology – Technology-based financial systems, digital banking, and automated finance.
  • BBA Digital Marketing – Online marketing, social media, SEO, content strategy, and branding.
  • BBA E-Commerce – Online business models, marketplaces, logistics, and digital operations.
  • BBA Supply Chain Management – Logistics, inventory control, and global supply systems.
Eligibility & Course Duration
  • Passed 12th Commerce (some colleges allow any stream)
  • Mathematics preferred for Analytics, FinTech & Data-related courses
  • Course Duration: 3 Years
Subjects You Will Commonly Study
  • Business Analytics & Data Basics
  • Digital Business Models
  • Financial Technology & Systems
  • Digital Marketing & SEO
  • E-Commerce Operations
  • Supply Chain & Logistics
  • Business Intelligence Tools
Who Should Choose Modern Commerce Degrees?
  • Students interested in technology + business
  • Those who want future-oriented and high-growth careers
  • Students comfortable with digital tools and data
  • Those who want private sector and startup opportunities
Career Options After Completion
  • Business Analyst
  • Digital Marketing Executive
  • FinTech Analyst
  • E-Commerce Manager
  • Supply Chain Executive
  • Data & Reporting Analyst
Private Sector Scope

Graduates are in demand in startups, IT companies, e-commerce platforms, digital marketing agencies, FinTech firms, multinational companies, and consulting organizations.

Government Job Scope

Government roles are limited compared to traditional commerce, but graduates can still appear for general competitive exams (SSC, Banking, UPSC) with additional preparation.

Average Salary (India)
  • Freshers: ₹4 – 7 LPA
  • With experience & skills: ₹8 – 15+ LPA
  • High growth in private and startup sectors
Higher Study & Skill Growth
  • MBA (Analytics, Digital Business, FinTech)
  • Advanced analytics & digital certifications
  • International business & technology programs
Skills You Must Develop
  • Data understanding & analytical thinking
  • Digital tools & software knowledge
  • Problem-solving and adaptability
  • Business + technology mindset

“Modern commerce degrees prepare students for the future of business.”
