Bachelor of Design (B.Des)
📌 Basic Introduction

Bachelor of Design (B.Des) is a professional undergraduate degree focused on design thinking, creativity, and problem-solving. It prepares students to design products, interfaces, visuals, and user experiences.

📋 Key Details
  • Degree: Bachelor of Design (B.Des)
  • Duration: 4 Years
  • Stream: Design & Innovation
  • Mode: Full-Time
  • Focus: Creativity + design skills
🎨 Specializations
  • Product Design
  • Communication Design
  • Interaction Design (UI/UX)
  • Industrial Design
✅ Eligibility
  • Passed 12th (any stream)
  • PCM students get advantage in technical design
  • Creative aptitude required
📝 Entrance Exams
  • UCEED
  • NID DAT
  • NIFT (some institutes)
  • University-level design exams
💼 Career Options
  • Product Designer
  • UI/UX Designer
  • Graphic Designer
  • Design Consultant
  • Creative Lead
💰 Salary Info
  • Freshers: ₹3 – 6 LPA
  • Mid-Level: ₹8 – 15 LPA
  • Senior: ₹18+ LPA
🏛️ Government Scope

Limited but available in public design units, government projects, teaching roles, and smart-city initiatives.

🏢 Companies / Work Areas
  • Design studios
  • IT & product companies
  • Startups
  • Freelancing & self-employment
⚖️ Private vs Government Comparison
Aspect Private Government

Creativity High Low
🎓 Internship Importance

Computer Science Engineering (CSE)
📌 What is Computer Science Engineering?

Computer Science Engineering (CSE) is a branch of engineering that focuses on computers, software, programming, data, and digital technologies. It involves designing, developing, and maintaining software systems, applications, and computer-based solutions.

  • Strong basics of Mathematics
  • Programming and coding skills
  • Analytical and algorithmic thinking

Students who enjoy coding, technology, computers, software development, problem-solving, and logical thinking should choose Computer Science Engineering.
