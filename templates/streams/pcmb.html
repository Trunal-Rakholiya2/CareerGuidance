Why Choose PCMB?
  • Keeps both medical and engineering options open
  • Best for students confused between PCB and PCM
  • Allows entry into research and emerging science fields
  • Provides maximum career backup options
Who Should Choose PCMB?
  • Students who can handle a heavy syllabus
  • Students with good time and stress management
  • Those interested in science, logic, and biology
Not recommended if you struggle with time management.
Subjects & Exams

Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology

Entrance Exams:

  • NEET (Medical)
  • JEE Main / Advanced (Engineering)
  • CUET (University Programs)
  • IISER / NISER (Research)
